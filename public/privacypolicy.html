Privacy

Headache Tracker is a personal diary. Everything you log is stored on this phone.

Health data

If you connect Health Connect, the app reads sleep, exercise, steps, resting heart rate, water and calories. It only reads. It never writes to Health Connect.

That data is saved in your diary on this phone, and in backups you make yourself or that Android makes to your own Google account. It is not sent anywhere else.

Weather

To log the weather, the app sends your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) to Open-Meteo. Nothing else is sent.
